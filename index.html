Estudios de Mercado I · Tema 3 — Focus group worksheet

What you do: run a short focus group on ONE topic, record it, get the recording transcribed, then apply thematic analysis to the transcript — themes, a map, and a decision for the client. Real primary data, captured and analysed in one session.

The topic — the student night out. Client: a new events / nightlife app that wants to win the student segment. Research question: how do students decide where to go out — the pull of the music, the venue, the people and the budget — and what makes a night feel “worth it”? The decision the client faces: what should the app offer to become students' default way to plan a night out?
Moderator's guide — a funnel, ~15–18 min (3.2 / 3.3). Keep it on topic without leading it; make it safe to disagree; don't let one voice dominate.
  1. Warm-up (2m): “Tell us about your last good night out.” (no wrong answers)
  2. Broad (4m): “Walk us through a typical Thursday/Friday — how do you decide where to go, and with whom?”
  3. Core (6m): “What makes a place worth staying at? How much does the music matter vs the people vs the price? Pick between two plans out loud.”
  4. Probe / ladder (3m): on the top thing someone names, ask “and why does that matter to you?” two or three times.
  5. Close (2m): “If someone built the perfect night-out app for students, what would it do?”
▸ Professor: organise the room — paste the class list, make the groups

~20 students → at least 6 recorded groups in an hour: two rounds of three groups, reshuffled, so everyone takes part twice and roles rotate. Paste one name per line.
